What the app stores on your device

When you run an auction, the teams, players, base prices, photos, logos and results you enter are saved in your browser’s storage on your own device. They are not sent anywhere unless live viewing is on.

Organizer accounts

Organizers can create an account with an email address and password, or with Google, through Google Firebase Authentication. Their auctions (teams, players, photos, logos, prices and results) are saved to that account so they can open them on any device. Only the organizer can see this saved data. Deleting an auction in the app removes it.

Player sign-up

When an organizer shares a sign-up link, players can enter their name, age, role and an optional photo. These details go to Google Firebase and only the organizer of that auction can see them until they add the player to the auction. Declined sign-ups are deleted.

What is shared when live viewing is on

To let people watch an auction live, the app copies the league name, team names, colours and logos, player names, roles, ages, prices and results, and the photo of the player currently up for bidding to Google Firebase. Anyone using the app can see this information, so only enter details the players are happy to have shown.

The app signs your device in to Firebase anonymously so that only the device that created an auction can change it. This uses a random ID. Apart from what organizers and players type in themselves, we don’t collect names, email addresses, phone numbers or location, and the app has no ads or tracking.

Watching an auction

Watching an auction doesn’t require an account and doesn’t store anything about you.

Deleting data

Choosing “Start a new league” in the app deletes the live copy of your auction from Firebase. You can turn live viewing off at any time with “Hide from viewers”. To ask for data to be removed, contact us below.
